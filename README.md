# meragharscheme.pk
Owning a home remains one of the biggest financial goals for families across Pakistan. However, rising property prices and high financing costs often make homeownership difficult for low and middle-income households. To address this challenge, the Government of Pakistan, in collaboration with the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), continues to support affordable housing initiatives through the [Mera Ghar Mera Ashiana Loan Scheme](https://meragharscheme.pk/) 2025.
